Australian Classification Board Symbols

Advisory Ratings (No Legal Restrictions)
G - General
Content is very mild in impact. Suitable for everyone.
PG - Parental Guidance
Content is mild in impact. May require parental guidance.
M - Mature
Content is moderate in impact. Not recommended for children under 15.
Restricted Ratings (Legal Restrictions Apply)
MA 15+ - Mature Accompanied
Content is strong in impact. Legally restricted to people aged 15 and over.
R 18+ - Restricted
Content is high in impact. Legally restricted to adults 18 years and over.
X 18+ - Restricted
Sexually explicit content. Legally restricted to adults 18 years and over.
