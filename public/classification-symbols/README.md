# Australian Classification Board Symbols

This directory contains the official Australian Classification Board symbols for films, computer games, and publications.

## Available Symbols

### Advisory Ratings (No Legal Restrictions)
- **G (General)** - Content is very mild in impact, suitable for everyone
- **PG (Parental Guidance)** - Content is mild in impact, may require parental guidance
- **M (Mature)** - Content is moderate in impact, not recommended for children under 15

### Restricted Ratings (Legal Restrictions Apply)
- **MA 15+ (Mature Accompanied)** - Content is strong in impact, legally restricted to people aged 15 and over
- **R 18+ (Restricted)** - Content is high in impact, legally restricted to adults 18 years and over
- **X 18+ (Restricted)** - Sexually explicit content, legally restricted to adults 18 years and over

## File Formats

- **EPS** - Original Encapsulated PostScript vector format (high quality, scalable)
- **PNG** - Web-friendly raster format (200x200 pixels, 300 DPI)

## Usage Guidelines

These symbols are official Australian Classification Board markings and should be used in accordance with the Board's guidelines:

1. **Accurate Representation** - Use the exact symbols without modification
2. **Proper Sizing** - Maintain appropriate proportions for visibility
3. **Clear Display** - Ensure symbols are clearly visible and readable
4. **Legal Compliance** - Follow all applicable laws and regulations
