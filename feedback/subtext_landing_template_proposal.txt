## 2) The Problem (3 pain points)

1. Shelf placement can be misleading
BookTok and retail “YA” labels don’t always reflect explicit content.

2. Classroom risk is hard to assess quickly
Teachers need confidence before assigning or recommending texts.

3. Parents need context before buying
Blurb/reviews often miss the practical suitability concerns.

---

## 3) How We Solve This (feature + benefit)

Quick Scan (15–30s)
Get immediate warning triage for first-pass decision making.

Deep Scan (90–120s)
Get richer context and higher-confidence insights before final decisions.

Clear “Why” Explanations
Understand why something is flagged, not just that it is flagged.
