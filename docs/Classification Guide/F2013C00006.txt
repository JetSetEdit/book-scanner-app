Clause 1

National Classification Code
1. Classification decisions are to give effect, as far as possible, to the
following principles:
(a) adults should be able to read, hear, see and play what they want;
(b) minors should be protected from material likely to harm or disturb
them;
(c) everyone should be protected from exposure to unsolicited material
that they find offensive;
(d) the need to take account of community concerns about:
(i) depictions that condone or incite violence, particularly sexual
violence; and
(ii) the portrayal of persons in a demeaning manner.

Publications
2. Publications are to be classified in accordance with the following table:

Description of publication

Classification
