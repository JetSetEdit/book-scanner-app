# Taxonomy Manifesto

## One-Sentence Version

> **"We provide transparent, neutral content warnings so readers can make informed choices about what they read, without judgment or moralizing."**

---

## Extended Version

We believe readers have the right to know what they're reading.

This taxonomy exists to help people avoid **surprise harm**, not avoid books.

We provide:
- **Transparency** - Clear, descriptive warnings
- **Neutrality** - No moral judgments, no culture-war language
- **Choice** - You decide what matters to you
- **Respect** - For all readers, regardless of their limits

We are not:
- Telling you what to read
- Judging what you read
- Censoring content
- Creating a moral framework

We are:
- Providing information
- Enabling informed choice
- Respecting reader agency
- Building a navigation system

---

## Why This Exists

**For BookTok readers:**
- Find books you want to read
- Avoid triggers you need to avoid
- Make informed choices with specificity

**For parents:**
- Quick "is this okay?" checks
- Clear, non-judgmental information
- Respect for your parenting decisions

**For librarians and educators:**
- Clarity, not vibes
- Practical, not academic
- Tools to help readers, not restrict them

---

## Our Promise

1. **We will be descriptive, not prescriptive**
   - "Contains depictions of..." not "Problematic"

2. **We will be neutral, not moralizing**
   - No judgment, no culture-war language

3. **We will be practical, not academic**
   - How real people talk, not jargon

4. **We will respect choice**
   - You decide what matters
   - We just provide the information

---

## Response to Common Criticisms

### "This is too much"
- It's optional - you choose to view warnings
- It's descriptive - we're not telling you what to read
- It's transparent - you see exactly what's flagged

### "This ruins reading"
- We help avoid *surprise harm*, not avoid books
- Many readers use warnings to *find* books they want
- Warnings enable choice, not restriction

### "This is censorship"
- We're not removing content, we're adding information
- Transparency is not censorship
- Readers have a right to know

### "This is overprotective"
- Different people have different limits
- What's mild for you might be severe for someone else
- We respect all limits, no judgment
