# Scans Per Dollar Analysis

## Quick Answer

**From $1, you can get approximately:**
- **GPT-4o only**: ~32 scans
- **Gemini only**: ~1,400 scans (or FREE within free tier)
- **Multi-model**: ~31 scans (or ~32 if Gemini is free)

## Detailed Breakdown

### GPT-4o Only
- **Cost per scan**: $0.031
- **Scans per $1**: $1 ÷ $0.031 = **32.26 scans**
- **Scans per $10**: ~323 scans
- **Scans per $100**: ~3,226 scans

### Gemini Only (Paid)
- **Cost per scan**: $0.0007
- **Scans per $1**: $1 ÷ $0.0007 = **1,429 scans**
- **Scans per $10**: ~14,286 scans
- **Scans per $100**: ~142,857 scans

### Gemini Free Tier
- **Daily limit**: 1,500 scans/day
- **Monthly limit**: ~45,000 scans/month (1,500 × 30)
- **Cost**: **FREE** (no cost for first 1,500 scans/day)

### Multi-Model (GPT-4o + Gemini)

**If Gemini is FREE (within free tier):**
- **Cost per scan**: $0.031 (only GPT-4o costs)
- **Scans per $1**: $1 ÷ $0.031 = **32.26 scans**

**If Gemini is PAID (after free tier):**
- **Cost per scan**: $0.032
- **Scans per $1**: $1 ÷ $0.032 = **31.25 scans**
